# SabayCode Brand Guidelines & Logo Redesign System

## 1. Brand Identity Overview
SabayCode is a bilingual (Khmer/English) developer education and SaaS platform. The brand identity balances trustworthy technical rigor with modern, accessible edtech design.

---

---

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Wordmark Display**: Geometric Sans-Serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Poppins` / `Inter`, 800 ExtraBold/Black, -0.03em tracking)
- **Tagline / Subtitle**: Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`, 700 Bold, Uppercase with `0.2em` wide tracking)
- **Body & Controls**: Sans-Serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)

---

## 5. Usage Rules & Guidelines
- **Icon Mark**: Simple geometric composition combining code brackets `< >` and a central stylized 'S' curve representing bilingual flow.
- **Color Restrictions**: Maximum 2 colors in the mark (`#2563EB` and `#FFFFFF` / `#93C5FD`). No gradients, drop shadows, 3D bevels, or mascots.
- **Minimum Clear Space**: Maintain at least 16px padding around horizontal lockups in navigation headers and footers.
